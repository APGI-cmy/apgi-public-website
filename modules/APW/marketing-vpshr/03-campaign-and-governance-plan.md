# APW VPSHR Launch — Marketing and Governed Delivery Plan v0.1
**Status:** Proposed; documentation-only scope.

## Positioning
**Master brand:** APGI — Loss prevention through operational maturity.
**Training proposition:** Practical security and human rights capability-building under the Voluntary Principles on Security and Human Rights.
**Course messaging:** Name the verified course and level exactly; distinguish general VPSHR promotion from a specific open-for-enrolment course.

## Suggested LinkedIn programme (four thematic weeks; dates only after readiness approval)
1. Why operational security and human rights belong together: human impacts, trust and operational continuity.
2. Responsibilities: employers, security personnel, contractors, managers and reporting/escalation.
3. Practical scenarios: access control, public-security coordination, proportionality, dignity and incident reporting; no confidential case claims.
4. Training invitation: course fit, outcomes, availability status and appropriate landing CTA.

Each post must have: campaign ID, source/course evidence, channel, audience, copy, approved claim list, graphic provenance, landing URL, approval owner/time, scheduled/published status and metric note. No automatic LinkedIn publishing unless explicitly authorised and technically validated.

## Proposed public landing copy
**Headline:** Security and Human Rights Training for Real Operational Decisions
**Subheadline:** Build the practical awareness and judgement teams need to apply the Voluntary Principles on Security and Human Rights in complex operating environments.
**Body:** APGI's VPSHR pathway supports security personnel, contractors, supervisors and organisations seeking to align daily security practices with dignity, accountability and responsible risk management. Explore the applicable level, delivery options and training outcomes.
**CTA before verified launch:** Request VPSHR training information.
**CTA only after verified enrolment readiness:** View course and enrol.
Avoid asserting unverified certification, accreditation, pricing, participant testimonials, course availability or guaranteed compliance.

## LinkedIn company page proposals
Tagline: **Loss prevention, operational maturity, security assurance and human rights training.**
About content must retain APGI's wider consulting and maturity ecosystem, not position APGI exclusively as a training business. Include the canonical VPSHR landing link only after publication and validation. Exact company LinkedIn URL, CTA permissions, banner and profile text require an authenticated page review or owner-provided screenshots.
